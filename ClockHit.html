Clock Hit

Description

Clock Hit is a single-player reverse boss battle game where time rewinds after you defeat the boss. To survive, you must catch the bullets you originally fired as they travel back toward you, reversing the battle that led to the boss's defeat.

Project Info

  • Team size: 7
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
  • Tools: Github, Blender
  • Duration: 2 days

  • Theme: TimeTravel & Choice
